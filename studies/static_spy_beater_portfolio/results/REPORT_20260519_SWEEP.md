# Static SPY-Beater Portfolio - 2026-05-19 Sweep Report

## Scope

Discovery-only sweep across all registered universes and all nine fitness families, plus focused incumbent refinements. These results do not validate or authorize any portfolio. Any promotion would require downstream PBO/DSR/bootstrap/WF/OOS validation and trial accounting `[advances_fin_ml, p.208-211]`, `[advances_fin_ml, p.222-223]`.

## Key Findings

- The unconstrained return/wealth objectives repeatedly converge to leveraged Nasdaq plus leveraged long-duration Treasuries: mostly `TQQQSIM/TMFSIM`, sometimes with `RSSTSIM`, `GDESIM` or `UGLSIM` depending on date window and universe.
- Focused refinement confirmed the broad-GA incumbents:
  - `core_1986`: `60% TMFSIM / 40% TQQQSIM`.
  - `mf_1988`: `50% TMFSIM / 35% TQQQSIM / 15% RSSTSIM`.
- Risk-focused fitnesses produce completely different portfolios: cash/bonds/KMLM-heavy for Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar, and `SPYSIM` itself for `mf_1988 min_regret`.
- The high-return candidates have extreme drawdowns (`~ -75%` to `-97%` depending window/universe). They are useful frontier diagnostics, not deployable portfolios.

## Interpretation

The current objective set is doing what it was designed to do: identify the historical frontier for each metric. The frontier is not a single portfolio:

- `relative_wealth_spy`, `relative_wealth_qqq`, `cagr_robust`, and `balanced_spy_beater` favor aggressive leveraged barbells.
- `sharpe_robust`, `sortino_robust`, and `calmar_robust` favor defensive mixes with cash/bonds/KMLM and low terminal wealth.
- `min_regret` favors benchmark-like stability and can choose `SPYSIM` itself.

The next useful research branch is not more unconstrained GA. It is a constrained/diversified family: max per asset/family, explicit MDD penalty, or separate objectives that require acceptable worst-window behavior while preserving relative wealth.
