# Decoded signal — OLD Happy News v1.4.1 (id 1612420)

## Family rationale

The system's vendor-supplied name ("Happy News") and timing fingerprint together
point to a clock-anchored, economic-release-driven strategy rather than any of
the predefined session families. Three independent pieces of evidence converge:

1. **Timing peaks are minute-level sharp, not session-distributed.**
   Stage 1 reports 356 of 788 trades (~45%) at exactly **15:30 UTC** (a single
   5-minute bucket), with secondary peaks at 17:00 UTC (95) and 11:30 UTC (72).
   These are not "session" hours — 15:30 UTC = 10:30 ET (winter) / 11:30 ET
   (summer DST), exactly the half-hour following the **10:00 ET US data slot**
   (ISM, JOLTS, Consumer Confidence, Existing Home Sales). 11:30 UTC ≈ 12:30 CET
   (post-ECB / German data window), and 17:00 UTC = noon ET (Treasury auctions,
   mid-session US data). A genuine session-overlap fader would be flatly
   distributed across 12-16 UTC; this distribution is bimodal and clock-locked
   to release schedules, which Brown describes as the source of "fillable-price
   gaps" in `[trading_systems_methods, p.743]`.

2. **Direction is not a function of the clock.** Buy% per top hour is 50.4%
   (15:00), 45.8% (17:00), 47.4% (11:00), 50.0% (04:00) — i.e., essentially
   coin-flip. A LONDON_OPEN_MOMENTUM or NY_SESSION_REVERSAL system would show
   a clear directional bias at the entry hour. Here the direction is decided
   by the macro tape state at release time (ret_3_H4 sign, dominant tree split
   with importance 0.52). This matches Chan's PEAD analogue
   `[algo_trading_chan, p.158, ch.7]`: position is taken in the **direction
   of the move already in progress** when the release confirms.

3. **The ret_3_H4 split is a momentum filter, not a reversal trigger.**
   Tree rank 1 (CV match 0.649) goes BUY when ret_3_H4 > 0 with a fan of
   confirmations (ema_dist_20_M15, bb_pos_20_2_M5) and SELL otherwise. Univariate
   ret_3_H4 > -0.000868 ⇒ Buy (CV 0.666, p_corr 1.7e-18) reinforces this. This
   is exactly the structure Chan recommends in `[algo_trading_chan, p.95]`:
   "drops caused by negative news are less likely to revert than those caused
   by liquidity demands" — i.e., when news prints in the direction of the H4
   trend, ride it; when it prints against, fade is unsafe. Not a session-overlap
   range trade.

**Alternatives considered and rejected:**

- `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE`: window 12-16 UTC overlaps the 15:30 peak, USD
  majors fit, exit is time-based — *but* this family implies BB-position
  fading inside the range, which the candidate ruleset does not show
  (bb_pos_20_2_H1 > 0.087 ⇒ BUY is a momentum-extension breakout, not fade).
  Forcing this label would mislead the replicator into trading mean-reversion.
- `NY_SESSION_REVERSAL`: 12-16 UTC fits, but again ret_3_H4 momentum-following
  is the opposite of reversal.
- `LONDON_OPEN_MOMENTUM` / `LATE_NY_BREAKOUT`: timing peaks (11/15/17) do not
  match the 06-09 or 21-01 windows.
- `FACTOR_SCALPING`: distribution is not flat — 15:30 dominates.
- `MARTINGALE_GRID`: sanity PASS, lot p95/p50=1.54 (no martingale).

`UNCATEGORIZED` is the honest call because the taxonomy lacks a NEWS_RELEASE
class. A future hardening of the taxonomy should add one (sub-label
"NEWS_RELEASE_MOMENTUM").

## Rule derivation

The rule is built by composing the most statistically significant Stage 1
findings:

- **Entry window 15:25-15:35 UTC**: directly from the empirical peak
  (356/788 trades = 45.2% of all entries land in the 15:30 5-minute bucket).
  Replicator can experiment with widening to 15:00-17:00 UTC to capture the
  secondary peak at 17:00, but the dominant edge is 15:30.
- **Pair universe**: all four pairs in the system (EURUSD 272, GBPUSD 227,
  AUDUSD 165, USDJPY 124). All quote-USD or USD-base, consistent with US
  releases at 10:00 ET driving the cross.
- **Direction primary gate**: `ret_3_H4 > -0.0008685` ⇒ BUY (Stage 1 univariate
  rank 3, CV 0.666, raw p = 3.1e-21, BH-corrected p = 1.7e-18 across n_tests=538).
  This is overwhelmingly significant after multiple-comparison correction
  per `[evidence_based_ta, p.367-380]` and is also the dominant tree split
  (importance 0.52, more than 5x any other feature).
- **Direction secondary confirm**: `bb_pos_20_2_H1 > 0.08711` ⇒ BUY (univariate
  rank 8, CV 0.670, BH-corrected p = 2.0e-19) and `ret_10_M5 <= 0` (tree's
  M5-pullback gate before BUY in the H4-positive branch — entry on a small
  M5 retrace within an H4 uptrend = textbook Chan-style PEAD continuation
  `[algo_trading_chan, p.158, ch.7]`).
- **SELL branch**: tree-derived. When `ret_3_H4 <= 0` AND `ema_dist_20_H1 > -2.82`
  (i.e., not extreme bearish stretch where reversal risk dominates), SELL.
  When ema_dist_20_H1 <= -2.82 the tree predicts class 0 in BOTH branches,
  so we abstain (NONE) under that extreme-stretch condition.
- **Exit**: Stage 1 hold percentiles are NaN — the tracker did not record
  hold durations cleanly. Exit_kind 100% manual_or_time means it was *not*
  a TP/SL hit (would show in pip distribution). I leave TP/SL nulls and let
  Stage 3 sweep `max_holding_hours ∈ {0.5, 1, 2, 4, 24}` to find the best
  fit. News-event PEAD literature suggests sub-day exits
  `[algo_trading_chan, p.153, ch.6]`: "Duration of momentum effects gets
  progressively shorter ... barely until the market closes."
- **Sizing**: lot p95/p50 = 1.54 is consistent with proportional-to-equity
  growth (equity drifted from $6.8k deposits + $14.6k profit ≈ 3x), not
  martingale or grid (martingale flag PASS, max_streak=0). Default is

  mean genuinely news-conditional, OR it could mean the vendor curated a

  vs SELL-only vs combined PnL to detect curation.

  score must include a stress with 2x normal slippage to gauge edge survival.
- **Demo bias**: live-fill divergence on news-release strategies is the
  single biggest gap between published track records and reality. Even if
  replicator matches PnL within ±10%, the live-translation reliability is
  still capped low until a sample of cash trades exists.
