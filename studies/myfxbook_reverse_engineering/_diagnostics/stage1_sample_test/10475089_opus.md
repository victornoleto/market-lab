  drift.

- `NY_SESSION_REVERSAL` / `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE` — entry windows
  12-16 UTC; don't apply.

Given (a) the taxonomy gap for Tokyo/JPY-cross fixed-time entries, (b) the
