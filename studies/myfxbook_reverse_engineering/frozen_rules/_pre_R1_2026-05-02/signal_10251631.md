# Decoded signal — Happy Gold FM - REAL (GN) (id 10251631)

## Family rationale

This system trades exclusively XAUUSD (gold) on a real ForexMart account at 1:500 leverage. It does not trade any FX pair, which immediately disqualifies it from the core families `LATE_NY_BREAKOUT`, `LONDON_OPEN_MOMENTUM`, `LONDON_OPEN_MR`, `NY_SESSION_REVERSAL`, and `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE` — all of which are defined around FX majors with USD/EUR. The `OVERNIGHT_GAP_FADE` family also does not fit because the timing is intraday and not concentrated at weekend boundaries.

The timing distribution shows 115 trades at 03:00 UTC, 80 at 04:00, 52 at 02:00, and 50 at 01:00 — the 01-06 UTC band accounts for approximately 84% of all entries. This corresponds to the late Asian session / early London pre-market for gold, a period of thin liquidity before the London open at 08:00 UTC. The direction is near-perfectly balanced (Buy 50.3%, Sell 49.7%), which is either evidence that the system uses a symmetric signal (same indicator, opposite thresholds) or that direction itself is nearly irrelevant and the edge comes from a timing/volatility effect.

`FACTOR_SCALPING` is the least-bad classification: the system uses intraday features (bb_pos, ema_dist, close_vs_session_open at M1/M15/H1/H4 granularities) on a single instrument, and the lot spread p95/p50=1.22 indicates approximately fixed sizing. However, the hold-duration data is entirely missing (all nan), and the match_rate_cv of the best direction rule is only 0.611 at 54% coverage — barely above the 0.503 all-Sell baseline. This is substantially weaker than what would be expected for a strategy with real edge. For that reason, confidence is set at 0.38, below the 0.50 threshold that would normally trigger UNCATEGORIZED.

The `UNCATEGORIZED` family was seriously considered but rejected on the grounds that FACTOR_SCALPING is at least a valid description of the mechanical structure: single-instrument, intraday features, distributed entry timing, apparent flat sizing. What is genuinely unknown is whether any directional signal exists at all, or whether the P&L is driven purely by spread/timing effects in the 01-06 UTC window.

The 5-minute resolution shows 04:00 (20 trades), 01:00 (19 trades), 03:05 (16 trades), 03:00 (15 trades) — no single clock-on-the-hour domination as seen in the HMH v2.3.1 pilot, suggesting the entry is triggered by a condition, not a cron-like schedule.

**Direction signal — univariate evidence**: The rank-8 rule `close_vs_session_open_H4 > -1 => Sell` is the only candidate with a Bonferroni-corrected p-value below 0.001 (p_corr=0.000477). It has match_rate_cv=0.611 at coverage=0.537. The interpretation is: when the H4 close has not fallen more than 1 full unit below the H4 session open (i.e., price is near or above the session open), the system is more likely to be entering Short. This is a weak mean-reversion signal — when gold has not dropped significantly from the H4 session open, the system fades any small positive move.

**Direction signal — tree evidence**: The rank-1 tree assigns feature importance 0.37 to `close_vs_session_open_H4`, consistent with the univariate result. The primary split at `<= -0.50` separates two regimes: (a) when the H4 close is more than 0.50 units below the H4 session open (strong intra-session decline), the tree assigns BUY unless M15 BB position is above 0.05; (b) when H4 is near or above session open, the tree almost uniformly assigns SELL regardless of subsidiary conditions. This is a classic mean-reversion structure on the H4 timeframe: fade strong moves, follow smaller ones. However, the overall CV accuracy of 0.531 with std=0.056 (fold range: 0.424 to 0.576) does not significantly exceed the 0.503 baseline.
