# Decoded signal — OLD Happy Market Hours v2.3.1 (id 1407880)

## Family rationale

Timing dominates the family decision. The fingerprint shows **3303 of 3304 trades** concentrated in the 23:00–01:00 UTC window (hour 23 = 1375, hour 00 = 1680, hour 01 = 248), with hour 22 contributing a single trade and hours 02–22 contributing zero. The 5-minute peaks (00:00 / 23:00 / 00:05 / 23:55 / 00:15) form a tight cluster straddling the 00 UTC boundary — exactly the late-New-York / pre-Asian crossover zone. The pair universe — GBPUSD, USDCAD, EURUSD, EURCHF, USDCHF, EURGBP — is the USD/EUR/GBP/CHF/CAD axis with no JPY-cross or commodity-FX exposure that would point to an Asian-session strategy. This is the canonical signature of the taxonomy's `LATE_NY_BREAKOUT` family ("Entry concentrado 21-01 UTC, exit 1-3h, FX majors com USD/EUR").

The literal direction logic, however, is **mean-reversion on M15 Bollinger Band position**, not breakout-of-Asian-range. The top tree rule (CV match_rate 0.640, MDI importance bb_pos_20_2_M15=0.62) buys when M15 price is in the bottom 15% of the BB envelope **and** H1 range is quiet (range_norm_H1 ≤ 0.72), and sells in essentially all other configurations. Functionally this is a fade of the late-NY price extension before Asian-session illiquidity, not momentum-with-breakout. I keep the system in `LATE_NY_BREAKOUT` because:

1. The taxonomy entry is keyed primarily on the 21–01 UTC window plus FX-majors universe — both match unambiguously.
2. The alternative `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE` requires 12–16 UTC entries (zero trades observed). `LONDON_OPEN_*` requires 06–09 UTC (zero trades). `OVERNIGHT_GAP_FADE` would concentrate on Friday-late / Monday-morning timestamps, but the 33.9-day max gap appears as multi-day pauses, not a weekly fade pattern. `MARTINGALE_GRID` is excluded by sanity PASS (max_streak=0).
3. The HMH (Happy Market Hours) vendor signature is publicly known to operate exactly the 23–01 UTC slot — this is the pilot known-good identity, and the timing match is not coincidence.

The within-family direction nuance (fade vs. breakout) is captured in the rule body, not by re-categorising. If the taxonomy ever splits LATE_NY into BREAKOUT and FADE sub-families, this system would move to FADE; for now, BREAKOUT covers the timing slot and the rule itself documents the direction logic.

## Rule derivation

Three pieces of evidence converge on the rule, with thresholds taken verbatim from `candidates.json`:

1. **Tree rank 1** (CV=0.640, std=0.054 across 5 folds, coverage=1.0). Feature importance: `bb_pos_20_2_M15=0.62`, `bb_pos_20_2_M5=0.26`, `range_norm_H1=0.12`. The tree paths translate cleanly to:

## Open questions (para Stage 3 + posteriores)

- **Realized hold distribution.** Stage 1's NaN hold percentiles must be recomputed from raw entry/exit timestamps. If p50 hold ≪ 6h, tighten the cap. If a fraction of trades holds > 6h, the time-exit is heterogeneous (possibly TP-driven, in which case `take_profit_pips: null` is wrong).
- **bb_pos threshold sensitivity.** Tree uses 0.15, univariate optimum is 0.1951. Test the rule at {0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25} on out-of-sample fold to estimate threshold stability — per `[evidence_based_ta, p.291]`, parameter sensitivity is a leading indicator of data-mining bias. Also test vol-adaptive variants (e.g. rolling quantile of bb_pos over 200 prior trades) since BB itself is vol-sensitive.
- **Per-pair direction calibration.** USDCHF (60% Sell), EURCHF (56% Sell), EURGBP (55% Sell) skew Sell more than EURUSD/GBPUSD (both ~52% Buy). The single-rule logic above ignores pair identity; Stage 3 should test whether per-pair multipliers add reliability beyond sample-size variance.
- **Hour-1 sell-bias.** buy_pct at hour 01 is 29.4% (vs 48.6% at hour 0, 51.1% at hour 23). The tree may capture this via BB features but Stage 3 should measure how much of the hour=01 sell-skew is explained by the Bollinger rule alone vs a residual time-of-entry interaction.
- **Full-sample re-mining.** Candidates were derived from `--sample 300` of 3304 trades. Re-running Stage 1 on full sample is the single highest-value replay before locking the rule.
- **Sizing calibration.** Regress lot vs rolling equity to estimate the exact risk-per-trade %. Lot p99/p50 = 4.42, so the proportional-2% guess may be off; could be 1.5% or 2.5%.
- **Calendar-coverage.** 33.9-day max gap implies non-trivial inactivity. Test whether trades are skipped on specific weekdays, holidays, or volatility regimes (DXY range expansion, NFP days, etc.).
- **Blackout 2021-06 → 2026-05.** Vendor abandoned this account 5 years ago. Even if the decoded rule replicates the 2013-2021 backtest perfectly, that says nothing about 2021-2026 forward edge — Stage 3 must include an OOS Dukascopy replay over 2021-2026 with the locked rule and report the decay separately.
