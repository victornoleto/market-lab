## Family rationale

System 10563761 trades **BTCUSD exclusively** (436/436 trades), active between
2024-05-13 and 2026-01-29. The existing taxonomy was designed for FX session families
(LATE_NY_BREAKOUT, LONDON_OPEN_*, NY_SESSION_REVERSAL, OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE,
OVERNIGHT_GAP_FADE). BTCUSD trades 24/7 and has no Asian-range or London-open structure
in the FX sense, so none of the FX families are structurally applicable.

The closest available bucket is **FACTOR_SCALPING**, defined as "entry distributed,
durations < 30min, edge typically vol-targeting or pair-trading intraday." This system
does NOT tick all boxes cleanly (durations are unknown, it is single-asset not
pair-trading), but the defining characteristics that ARE visible — entry concentrated
in a specific intraday window (15:00-20:00 UTC = US equity/crypto overlap session),
momentum indicator as primary direction signal (Bollinger Band position and EMA
distance, both trend-following), and a completely automated entry/exit — are more
consistent with FACTOR_SCALPING than with any of the FX breakout or reversal families.

`MARTINGALE_GRID` was ruled out immediately: lot p95/p50 ratio = 1.26 (well within
the k1_pass threshold), steps=0, max_streak=0. `OVERNIGHT_GAP_FADE` was ruled out
because the entry concentration is intraday (15-19 UTC), not at weekend open or
Friday close. `LATE_NY_BREAKOUT` requires FX majors and entry at 21-01 UTC — BTCUSD
at 15-19 UTC does not qualify. `LONDON_OPEN_*` requires 06-09 UTC. `NY_SESSION_REVERSAL`
and `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE` require FX context and 12-16 UTC with mean-reversion
or range-fade logic — this system's signal is strictly momentum (BB above midband = BUY),
not reversal.

The **15:00-20:00 UTC window** corresponds to the peak of US equity market trading
(NYSE opens 13:30 UTC) and the period of maximum Bitcoin spot volume as US retail
and institutional flows enter. Kaufman [trading_systems_methods, p.326-327] documents
the Bollinger breakout-momentum use case directly: "Buy on close > upper band; short
on close < lower band" — in this system the threshold is the midband (bb_pos > 0.23,
where 0 = midband, 1 = upper band), which is a less aggressive version of the same
momentum idea: enter long when price has already demonstrated upward displacement
from the 20-period mean.
