Three equal-mass clusters at exact 4-hour spacing read most plausibly as *manual/discretionary entry windows* (analyst review times, or H4-bar-open scans) rather than a programmed session-edge strategy. Sonnet's prior decode anchored on the H4-bar-open observation and tagged `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE` — but a 4h-grid pattern on a swing system is *consistent with manual review cadence*, not necessarily with an intraday range-trading family that requires sub-4h holds.
