# Decoded signal — Happy Breakout v1.0 (id 1603276)

## Family rationale

The system name "Happy Breakout v1.0" directly declares its mechanism. The entry hour
distribution shows 57 trades at 09:00 UTC, 72 at 10:00 UTC, and 47 at 11:00 UTC — a
cluster consistent with London open and early London session breakout entries
(06:00-12:00 UTC). A secondary cluster appears at 15:00-17:00 UTC (69+56+47 trades)
which aligns with the NY-London overlap and early NY session. The name "Breakout" plus
the dominant direction rule (`close_vs_session_open_H1 = 1.0` in RIPPER rank-2) signals
a momentum-following strategy: enter in the direction of how price has moved relative to
the session open.

`LONDON_OPEN_MOMENTUM` is preferred over `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE` because (a) the
earliest activity starts at 09:00 UTC (London open), (b) the RIPPER ruleset's most
frequent trigger is `close_vs_session_open_H1 = 1.0` which is a classic session-open
breakout condition, and (c) the Buy% by pair shows nearly balanced direction (EURUSD
53.5%, GBPUSD 51.0%) — consistent with momentum-following rather than range-fading. A
pure `OVERLAP_NY_LONDON_RANGE` family would show stronger directional signals in the
12-16 UTC window.

`NY_SESSION_REVERSAL` was considered but rejected: that family requires opposite
direction to the London move and peak at 12-16 UTC. Here the direction is NOT opposite
to trend; BUY is triggered when `ema_dist_20_H1 > -0.27` (price near or above EMA =
trending up), which is a continuation signal, not a reversal.

`FACTOR_SCALPING` was considered given the broad 09:00-17:00 UTC window and multiple
pairs, but the hold duration is not sub-30-minute (hold durations are NaN in the
fingerprint, suggesting multi-hour or day-long holds), and there is no vol-targeting
structure in the candidates.

The system trades EURUSD (68%), GBPUSD (17%), and USDJPY (12%) — all major USD pairs
with deep liquidity at London open, consistent with session-open breakout strategies
documented in the FX literature. XAUUSD (3%, 16 trades) is excluded from the replicator
pairs as an outlier with insufficient data and structural dissimilarity.
