## Family rationale

The system trades exclusively XAUUSD (Gold), 1024 trades over approximately 5 years (2021-06 to 2026-04), with no martingale detected. Entry concentrates in two time blocks: a London-session cluster at 10-11 UTC (159 trades combined) and a dominant NY-open/London-close cluster at 15-17 UTC (374 trades combined), accounting for ~52% of all activity. The overall buy/sell ratio is near-balanced at 53.1%/46.8%, ruling out a systematic direction bias independent of signal.

The combination of XAUUSD-only universe, bimodal session entries, and signal logic dominated by Bollinger Band position (bb_pos_20_2_H1 at importance=0.78 in the tree) and short-term returns (ret_10_H1, ret_10_H4, ret_10_M15) places this system in the **FACTOR_SCALPING** family rather than any of the session-specific breakout or reversal families. The taxonomy families LATE_NY_BREAKOUT, LONDON_OPEN_MOMENTUM, LONDON_OPEN_MR, NY_SESSION_REVERSAL, and OVERNIGHT_GAP_FADE are all defined around FX majors and specific session windows (not Gold), and each implies a single dominant entry window tied to a clean Asian/London/NY structure. XAUUSD sessions have distinct liquidity patterns: London open (08:00-09:00 UTC) and NY open (13:30-14:00 UTC) are the primary volume peaks, not the 15-17 UTC window observed here. The 15-17 UTC peak in Gold likely corresponds to the post-NY-open consolidation and London afternoon close — a period of reduced but persistent trend continuation.

The second candidate family considered was **NY_SESSION_REVERSAL** (entry 12-16 UTC, exit 1-3h, opposite London move). The timing partially overlaps, but the direction signal in the RIPPER ruleset (rank 2) contains `close_vs_session_open_M15=1.0` as the leading condition in most sub-rules, which indicates the system follows intraday momentum (price above session open => more likely to buy), not reversal of the London move. The univariate rules all show monotone positive threshold conditions (Buy above threshold for bb_pos, ema_dist, ret) — consistent with a momentum/trend-continuation regime, not a reversal.

FACTOR_SCALPING was ultimately chosen because: (a) the entry is distributed across multiple hours and two distinct session windows, not concentrated in a 1-2h spike; (b) the signal logic relies on multi-timeframe factor combinations (BB position on H1, returns on H4/M15) without a session-breakout structure; (c) the system name contains "M30" suggesting an indicator computed on 30-minute bars, consistent with intraday scalping decisions; and (d) Gold intraday momentum strategies driven by Bollinger Band and EMA distance are documented as factor-based in the literature — Chan notes in [algo_trading_chan, p.95, ch.4] that momentum filters (price above moving average) are used as gates to capture persistent directional moves rather than pure mean-reversion.
