## Family rationale

The fingerprint resists a clean fit to any of the FX-session families in the
closed taxonomy and, on the rule required for `FACTOR_SCALPING`, fails one
necessary condition: hold p50 < 30min cannot be verified — `hold` columns are
NaN across the trade list. Per the brief's hard constraint ("if not fit, use
UNCATEGORIZED — do NOT create a new label") and the explicit guidance from
Wave 1+2 reclassifications (FACTOR_SCALPING is being demoted whenever hold or
tree degeneracy fails), I select `UNCATEGORIZED` rather than force-fit.

That said, the underlying rule **is real and non-degenerate**:

- **Tree (rank 1)** has CV match_rate 0.871 against a baseline of 0.531
  (Always-Buy = 0.5312). That is +34pp of genuine lift, well above what a
  degenerate-baseline-clone would produce. The dominant feature
  `bb_pos_20_2_H1` carries importance 0.78 and the split at -0.10 produces
  a meaningful asymmetry: the right branch (bb_pos > -0.10) leaves are
  uniformly class 1 (Buy) across all sub-conditions on `ret_10_H4`,
  `ret_3_H1`, `ret_10_M5`, `ema_dist_20_H1` (≤1.64 OR >1.64 still Buy);
  the left branch (bb_pos ≤ -0.10) is mostly class 0 (Sell), with a single
  Buy pocket (ret_3_H1 ≤ 0 AND ret_10_H1 > -0.01).
- **Univariate confirms at multiple resolutions**: `bb_pos_20_2_H1 > 0.1347`
  (rank 5, 0.854 acc, p_corr 1e-122), `ema_dist_20_H1 > -0.3576` (rank 3,
  0.848 acc, p_corr 1e-118), `ret_10_H1 > -0.001147` (rank 4). All point to
  the **same H1 trend regime**: when gold is above its H1 BB midline / above
  its H1 EMA / has positive H1 momentum → BUY; otherwise → SELL.
- **CV folds stable**: [0.887, 0.858, 0.799, 0.917, 0.894], std 0.041, no
  collapse fold.

This is consistent with Kaufman's empirical rule for metals being trend-
follow markets [trading_systems_methods, p.13] and with the canonical
"Bollinger breakout" interpretation [trading_systems_methods, p.326-327]:
buy on close above the upper band, short below — applied at H1 resolution
on XAUUSD. It is **NOT** mean reversion: the sign of the trade tracks the
sign of bb_pos, not its mirror.

Why I considered and rejected each named family:

  Marginal fit, conf would be ~0.40. Considered, ranked second-best.

Given the rule has economic plausibility (Kaufman: metals trend-follow;

## Rule derivation

I drew the direction rule directly from the **tree (rank 1)**, not the
univariate, for three reasons:

1. **Higher CV match_rate** (0.871 vs 0.854 univariate top), and with full
   coverage 1.0 (univariate top has 0.50 coverage — only 50% of trades are
   bb_pos > 0.1347).
2. **Captures both branches**: a univariate-only rule would say "BUY when
   bb_pos > 0.13, abstain otherwise"; the tree exposes the SELL branch
   (bb_pos ≤ -0.10) which carries roughly half the trade volume.
3. **Stable across folds** (std 0.041), no degenerate baseline-clone
   behavior.

The Buy-pocket within the SELL branch (ret_3_H1 ≤ 0 AND ret_10_H1 > -0.01)
is a sub-leaf of the tree in the lower-band region. Its economic reading:
when H1 BB-position is below the midline AND short-term H1 return is non-
positive AND 10-bar H1 return is not deeply negative → a near-band bounce
is more likely than continuation. This is a small Hurst<0.5 island within
an otherwise momentum rule. I keep it in the rule but with **lower
confidence**: it's the smallest leaf and the most prone to drift.

I did **not** invent thresholds: -0.10 and -0.01 / 0.00 are verbatim from
the tree dump in `candidates.json`. The univariate threshold 0.1347 is
reported as an alternative confirmation, not used in the primary rule.

I deliberately **omitted** RIPPER (rank 2, 24 disjuncts): per
[advances_fin_ml, ch.5], a high-cardinality conjunctive rule with no
purged holdout is overfit-prone. Its CV match_rate is also lower
(0.809 vs tree 0.871).

Exit logic is the weakest piece. `exit_kind = manual_or_time` for all 1024
trades, but hold duration is `NaN` across the board — likely a Stage 1 join
failure on close timestamps, not a strategy attribute. Until Stage 3
reconstructs holding times from raw trade rows, I leave `max_holding_hours
= null` and flag this as a `risk_flag`. Stage 3 should grid-search {1h, 4h,
8h, 24h} time-stops plus an ATR-anchored TP/SL pair, since 25.95% drawdown
on a 8.65% monthly profile **requires** some risk control — pure naked
trend on M30 gold without stop would have blown up.

I diverge from the prior Sonnet decode in three concrete ways:

1. **Family**: Sonnet → FACTOR_SCALPING (conf 0.62). Mine → UNCATEGORIZED
   (conf 0.50). Reason: hold-duration unknown precludes the <30min
   FACTOR_SCALPING condition; taxonomy is closed; "M30" in vendor name is

2. **Direction rule**: Sonnet collapsed the tree into a `bb_pos > 0.1347

   coverage (univariate coverages 0.50 and 0.60 do not multiply cleanly

3. **Exit**: Sonnet hard-coded `max_holding_hours: 4` as a "conservative
   ceiling". Mine = `null` because there is no evidence in the fingerprint
