## Family rationale

This system does **not fit cleanly into any of the 7 standard intraday families** in the taxonomy.
The entry peak is 13:00 UTC (NY session open, 58.5% of trades), which superficially
suggests `NY_SESSION_REVERSAL` (12-16 UTC). However, `NY_SESSION_REVERSAL` requires exit
time-based 1-3h, and this system shows p50 hold = 24h and p95 hold = 504h (21 days). A
median hold of 24 hours is categorically a swing strategy, not an intraday session play.

The three intraday families (LONDON_OPEN_*, LATE_NY_BREAKOUT) all specify exit < 4h. The two
remaining candidates are `FACTOR_SCALPING` (entry distributed, duration < 30min) — ruled out
by the 24h median hold — and `OVERNIGHT_GAP_FADE` (Friday/Monday entry) — ruled out because
the entry is concentrated Monday-Friday at 13 UTC with no weekend gap logic detected.

The near-constant SELL direction (88% on both EURUSD and USDCHF) further disqualifies all
families that derive direction from session range or prior-bar sign, because the direction
signal here is nearly static. The `dow > 0 => Sell` univariate rule (candidates.json rank 4,
coverage 81.3%) means "sell on any weekday except possibly Sunday," confirming the structural
SELL bias is calendar-driven, not signal-driven.

EURUSD short + USDCHF short implies: short EUR, long USD (from EURUSD), and simultaneously
short USD, long CHF (from USDCHF). These positions partially cancel each other on USD and
instead express a combined **EUR-bearish / CHF-bullish** view, equivalent to a synthetic
EURCHF short. This is consistent with Carver's carry rule framework `[systematic_trading,
p.119 ch.7]`: "the carry rule buys assets with high expected carry and sells those with
negative carry." EUR historically exhibited lower carry than CHF during certain yield
regimes (2017-2021 covered by this account).

Alternatives considered and rejected:
- `NY_SESSION_REVERSAL`: timing matches but exit horizon (24h+) disqualifies
- `LATE_NY_BREAKOUT`: entry window (13 UTC) is outside 21-01 UTC definition
- `FACTOR_SCALPING`: hold times 24-504h far exceed the < 30min threshold
- `LONDON_OPEN_MOMENTUM`: entry peak 13 UTC is 4-5h outside the 06-09 UTC window
- `MARTINGALE_GRID`: k1_pass=PASS confirmed, lot ratio p95/p50 = 1.00 (no martingale)

Family assigned: `UNCATEGORIZED`. The system appears to implement a **persistent
directional swing** on EUR/CHF relative value with NY-open timing, a structure not
represented in the current taxonomy.
