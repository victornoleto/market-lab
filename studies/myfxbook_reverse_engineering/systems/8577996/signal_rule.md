## Family rationale

The system is classified as **MARTINGALE_GRID** based on convergent evidence from four independent diagnostic dimensions, without any ambiguity sufficient to justify an alternative taxonomy.

**Dimension 1 — Lot dynamics (k1 sanity, primary trigger).** The Stage 1 sanity check reports a martingale flag FAIL with `steps=22` and `per-month max/median P95 = 4.10`, exceeding the hard threshold of 3.0 that indicates within-month position doubling. The lot distribution (p50=0.01, p95=0.03, p99=0.04, max=0.06) combined with the p95/p50 ratio of exactly 3.0 confirms non-uniform lot scaling. In a pure signal-driven strategy, lot sizes would be proportional to equity or fixed; a 3.0× ratio between median and 95th percentile is a fingerprint of grid layering where open positions accumulate as price moves against the initial leg. Per the taxonomy instructions, `k1_pass=False` triggers immediate classification as MARTINGALE_GRID.

**Dimension 2 — Direction signal absence.** The best available candidates from three independent miners (tree, ripper, univariate) all fail to demonstrate statistically significant predictive power. Aronson's EBTA [p.281, p.345] explicitly states: "NEVER use single-rule back test p-values to evaluate the best rule from a data-mining run. Only tests that incorporate data-mining bias are valid." Every corrected p-value in candidates.json is either 0.182 (rank 4), 0.320 (rank 5), or 1.0 (ranks 6-10). A grid engine does not require a predictive signal — it opens opposing or cascading legs purely on price distance from existing positions, which produces the observed 50/50 BUY/SELL split by pair (AUDUSD: 49.8%, EURCHF: 50.3%, USDCHF: 49.9%).

**Dimension 3 — Hold duration distribution.** Intraday session families (LATE_NY_BREAKOUT, LONDON_OPEN_MOMENTUM, NY_SESSION_REVERSAL) have hold p50 of 1-4 hours with p95 under 24h. This is structurally impossible for a session-timed entry strategy with a defined exit rule. It is entirely consistent with a grid system holding losing legs open for weeks or months awaiting recovery, as described by Chan [algo_trading_chan, p.153-154]: "mean-reverting strategies have capped upside but potentially unbounded drawdown" — the asymmetry is maximized by grid systems that never close losing legs with a stop loss.
